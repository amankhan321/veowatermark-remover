■ Guide

Remove the Veo 3 watermark without uploading

What is the Veo watermark?

Videos generated with Google Veo 3 and the Gemini app carry a small visible logo in the bottom-right corner — a "Veo" text mark or the Gemini sparkle. Clearframe is a free Veo watermark remover that cleans that corner on every frame so your clip is ready for Shorts, Reels, X or an edit timeline.

Why a browser-based remover?

Most online watermark removers make you upload your video, wait in a queue and buy credits. This Gemini video watermark remover runs locally with your browser's own video engine: nothing is uploaded, there are no limits, and an 8-second Veo clip is done in about 8 seconds.

Works with Veo 3, Veo 3.1 and Gemini

Auto-detection looks for the overlay that stays fixed while the scene moves, so it handles text and logo marks at 720p, 1080p and vertical 9:16 clips. If a clip is very static, pick the Veo or Gemini preset or drag the box yourself.

Tips for the cleanest result

Use the original download from Gemini or Flow rather than a re-compressed copy, keep the box tight around the logo, and use Chrome or Edge on desktop for MP4 output. Busy textures behind the logo are rebuilt from the surrounding pixels.

■ FAQ

Questions

How does it work?

It grabs a dozen frames spread across your clip and measures which pixels keep the same sharp edges while everything else changes. Those pixels form the watermark mask. During export each frame is redrawn and the masked area is filled by diffusing colour in from the surrounding pixels, with a touch of grain so it blends in.

Is this Veo watermark remover free?

Yes. It is completely free with no sign-up, credits or limits, because all processing runs in your browser.

Does it work with Veo 3 and Gemini videos?

Yes. It auto-detects the Veo text watermark and the Gemini sparkle logo in the corner, and has presets for both.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. The page reads the file locally and records the cleaned result in memory. Close the tab and it's gone.

Why does it take as long as the clip?

The export plays the clip once in the background and records the cleaned frames plus audio as it goes. Veo clips are short, so it's usually a few seconds.

What format do I get?

MP4 on browsers that can record it (recent Chrome / Edge), otherwise WebM. Both upload fine to X, YouTube and Instagram — if one complains, run it through a converter.

Does this remove SynthID?

No. Google also embeds an invisible SynthID watermark in the pixels themselves; this tool only cleans the visible corner logo. Please label AI-generated content where platforms ask you to.

Detection says "low" — what now?

That happens on very static shots or heavily re-encoded files. Pick the Veo or Gemini preset, or drag the box over the logo yourself, then hit remove.
